# ARGUS Brand Guide

- **Name:** ARGUS
- **Descriptor:** AICTE Approval & Compliance Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline:** Complete Visibility. Accountable Governance.
- **Positioning:** proposed hackathon solution, not an official AICTE product or endorsement.

## Visual guidance

Use the provided logo and icon assets without stretching, skewing, or altering their proportions. Maintain adequate contrast and whitespace. Keep the project title descriptive in official submissions. Do not add the AICTE logo or imply affiliation unless the event/authority explicitly authorizes its use.

## Assets

- `argus-mark.svg` — symbol-only mark.
- `argus-logo-horizontal.svg` — horizontal wordmark for appropriate backgrounds.
- `argus-logo-horizontal.png` — PNG fallback.
- `prototype/assets/` contains the small SVG assets used by the supplied prototype.

## Tone

Institutional, precise, accountable, transparent. Prefer “configured automation”, “rule-driven follow-up”, and “human-reviewed decisions”. Avoid “fully autonomous approval”, “official AICTE integration”, or quantified impact claims without evidence.
