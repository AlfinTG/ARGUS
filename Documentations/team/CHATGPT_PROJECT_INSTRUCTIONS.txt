PRODUCT THESIS
ARGUS is automation-first. Its core loop is event -> validate state and authorization -> evaluate a configured rule -> prevent duplicate action -> perform a permitted task/routing/reminder/escalation -> write a traceable audit record -> expose the outcome to the responsible user. Dashboards support this loop; they are not the main innovation by themselves.

MANDATORY CAPABILITIES
- Configurable route profiles and route-aware stage movement, preconditions and invalid-transition blocking.
- Deficiency return requiring a meaningful reason and preserving the stage to resume after institutional response.
- Pendency, aging, current owner, due date and next action visibility.
- EVC eligibility queue, scheduling/assignment, visit and report status, reminder/escalation.
- Time-bound obligations and compliance evidence with separate submission and authorized verification states.
- Document expiry warnings, renewal submission and separate authorized review.
- Configurable reminders/escalations with idempotency keys so repeated event processing cannot duplicate actions in the same rule/threshold window.
- Audit trail with actor/system, timestamp, target, rule ID/version, reason, result and correlation/reference ID.
- Role-aware UI; production role enforcement must be server-side.
- Final scrutiny, inspection, verification and grant/reject decisions remain with authorized officials. Never let AI autonomously decide a statutory application.
