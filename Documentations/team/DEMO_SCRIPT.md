# 5-Minute ARGUS Demo Script

Use a browser reset and synthetic records before starting. Mention the simulation boundary upfront.

## 0:00–0:30 — Establish the need

“SIH26262 calls for integrated tracking of approval workflow stages, EVC scheduling, time-bound compliance, document validity, alerts and auditability. ARGUS makes the operational chain visible and rule-driven. This is a hackathon prototype with synthetic data, not an official AICTE system.”

## 0:30–1:30 — Route and deficiency loop

Open a case and identify route profile, stage, owner and due date. Complete a permitted stage and show the next stage comes from the selected route. Then return another case for deficiency: first show that no reason is rejected, then record the reason, submit a response and show the case resumes at the designated review point.

## 1:30–2:30 — Automation cycle

Open rules and explain the configured demo threshold. Run the cycle and show a due-soon reminder, overdue escalation, EVC scheduling task and document expiry warning. Run it a second time and show no duplicate side effects for the same rule/subject/window.

## 2:30–3:30 — EVC and compliance

Show an eligible EVC queue item, assign a visit date/panel, and keep report status separate. Submit evidence as the institution role, then change to the reviewer role and verify it. Emphasize that submission does not equal verification.

## 3:30–4:20 — Documents and audit

Show a document approaching expiry, submit a renewal and keep it pending review. Open audit history and trace workflow/action records to the source rule and actor.

## 4:20–5:00 — Boundaries and next steps

“ARGUS automates coordination and traceability; statutory scrutiny and final decisions remain with authorized officials. Real authentication, durable server-side storage, external notification delivery and authorized AICTE integration remain outside this browser simulation. Next, the team would harden the backend, test role isolation and validate every policy timeline against current authorized guidance.”
